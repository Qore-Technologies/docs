Qorelly helps you run your business from one place. Whether you own a restaurant, spa, pharmacy, retail store, hotel, rental business, or any kind of professional service, Qorelly brings everything together — your menu, orders, stock, bookings, customers, and team.

## How to use Qorelly

Here's the short version of how to use Qorelly, start to finish:

1. **[Sign up and set up your business](/quickstart)** — create your account and pick your business type
2. **[Turn on the features you need](/platform/apps-modules)** — Menu, POS, Inventory, Bookings, and more
3. **[Build your menu](/platform/menu-management)** — add categories, items, prices, and photos
4. **[Take orders or bookings](/platform/point-of-sale)** — on the web dashboard or the [mobile app](/mobile/overview)
5. **[Invite your team](/platform/team-management)** — give each person the right role and access

Prefer to watch instead of read? The [video tutorials](/video-tutorials) walk through every one of these steps.

## Why Qorelly?

- **Works for any business** — One platform that speaks the language of restaurants, spas, pharmacies, retail, hospitality, rentals, and professional services
- **Use it anywhere** — A web dashboard you can open in any browser, plus an app for your phone, tablet, iPad, or desktop computer
- **Everything in one place** — Take orders, track stock, manage bookings, keep up with customers, and run your team — without switching between apps
- **Multiple branches? No problem** — Manage all your locations from a single account
- **Your team, your rules** — Decide exactly who can see and do what with flexible roles and permissions
